# VOIDBREAKERS CONCEPT DOCUMENT

## Project Description
Voidbreaks is a sci-fi co-op survival looter game where players take on the roles of a scavenger crew exploring and salvaging derelict spacecraft. These massive, silent ships drift through the depths of space, seemingly lifeless but filled with danger. As players search for valuable items, they must work together to stay alive, avoid deadly threats, and find a way back to their own ship before it is too late.
Each mission begins aboard the players’ personal ship, which acts as their home and launching point. Instead of piloting through space, players use a control interface to locate a nearby target. Once selected, the abandoned vessel is generated with a unique layout, environmental conditions, and loot placement. This system ensures that every raid feels fresh and unpredictable, encouraging exploration and quick thinking.
The ships players explore are often damaged or unstable. Life support may be offline, requiring players to return to their own ship periodically to refill their oxygen supply. In some cases, radiation shielding may have failed, forcing the team to reroute power from their ship to activate protection. Other environmental challenges, like areas with no gravity, add additional complexity and danger during traversal.
Loot found within these ships includes vital survival gear and materials that can be used to craft weapons and tools. The crafting system is intentionally simple, allowing players to slowly build up their strength over time. As they gather resources and improve their equipment, players shift from stealth and avoidance to more direct confrontation. The number of enemies encountered depends on the size of the ship, making larger vessels more dangerous but also more rewarding.
The threats that inhabit these derelict ships are varied and dangerous. Players may run into rival AI-controlled scavenger crews, or encounter unknown creatures that were once contained aboard the vessel. These enemies do not fight each other and are entirely focused on hunting the players, creating a constant sense of tension and urgency.
Teamwork plays a crucial role in survival. If a player dies during a mission, they drop a data model that must be recovered by a teammate and brought back to the ship’s reconstruction station to revive them. If the entire crew is eliminated, all progress and collected items are lost, and the game resets to the beginning. To avoid leaving anyone behind, the ship cannot detach from a raid unless all alive players are safely on board.
Between missions, players can customize and upgrade their ship using a visual interface. They can choose where to place specific rooms that offer different functions and benefits, helping to prepare for future raids. The ship serves as the team’s only persistent base, and there are no additional locations or planets to manage. While there are currently no skill trees, perks, or specialized loadouts, the ability to expand and modify the ship gives players meaningful ways to progress and strategize.
Voidbreaks offers a challenging and immersive co-op experience built around survival, cooperation, and the unknown. With its mix of unpredictable environments, persistent risk, and tense exploration, it invites players to step into the shadows of space, work together, and find out what lies hidden within the void.

## Game Concept
Voidbreaks combines elements of survival, stealth, and looter gameplay in a dark sci-fi setting. Players begin each mission from a customizable base ship and use a graphical interface to locate derelict vessels to explore. Upon selection, a procedurally generated raid ship is created, with unique room layouts, loot placement, and environmental challenges. The game is designed for multiplayer co-op, requiring teamwork and strategic decision-making. Players must gather resources while dealing with enemies and environmental dangers, gradually building up their ability to confront more difficult missions. The tone is tense and immersive, grounded in themes of isolation, uncertainty, and adaptation.

## Gameplay Features
The game centers around a procedurally generated raiding system, where players board enemy vessels that are completely randomized in structure and content. Players face environmental hazards such as oxygen deprivation, radiation exposure, and lack of gravity, all of which affect movement and survival. Loot includes essential survival tools and crafting materials, which enable players to construct simple weapons and equipment over time. The game begins with a focus on stealth and avoidance, transitioning to combat as players grow stronger. Death in Voidbreaks has lasting consequences: if a teammate dies, their data model must be retrieved for revival, but if all players die, the game resets entirely, erasing all progress and collected loot.

## Narrative and World Design
Although Voidbreaks does not include traditional storytelling systems, it establishes a world rich in atmosphere and implied lore. Each derelict ship presents an opportunity for players to uncover fragments of what might have happened aboard. The origins of the monsters, the reasons behind the ships’ abandonment, and the source of the environmental decay are left intentionally ambiguous to support player curiosity and discovery. The absence of explicit narrative allows the game’s environments and enemy design to tell the story indirectly, encouraging players to form their own interpretations based on what they encounter during their missions.


## Visual and Audio Style
The visual design of Voidbreaks emphasizes dark, claustrophobic environments filled with flickering lights, debris, and damage that reflect a vessel in distress or decay. The interior of each ship is meant to feel hostile and unpredictable, visually reinforcing the constant threat of danger. The art direction leans into cold industrial sci-fi, with metallic corridors, sparse lighting, and eerie shadows. Audio plays a critical role in building tension, using ambient hums, distorted communication signals, and sudden enemy noises to immerse players in the experience. Sound cues also assist with gameplay, providing warnings about nearby enemies or environmental threats.

## Technical Plans
Voidbreaks is being developed in Unreal Engine 5.5, using the engine’s built-in multiplayer systems and procedural generation tools. The raid ships are assembled dynamically at runtime using modular level components, ensuring a variety of layouts and loot scenarios. Environmental conditions are managed through a set of systems that simulate oxygen levels, radiation exposure, and gravity effects. The player ship is a persistent structure, upgradeable between missions via a GUI that allows for placement of predefined room modules. The revival system, permadeath mechanic, and GUI-based navigation interface are central to gameplay and are built using Unreal’s blueprint and C++ systems.

## Development Roadmap
The project will begin with a pre-production phase focused on building core systems such as procedural ship generation, basic movement, and survival mechanics. Next, the development will focus on implementing environmental hazards, loot spawning, and simple AI for enemy encounters. Once these systems are stable, multiplayer functionality and revival mechanics will be added to support full co-op gameplay. The later stages will include ship customization, resource-based upgrades, and balancing of procedural content. Final polish will address user interface, sound design, and overall pacing. Post-launch improvements may include new types of enemies, additional environmental variations, and expanded mission functionality.


## Team and Roles
The project is currently being developed by a single developer overseeing all aspects of game design, programming, environment creation, and system integration. This structure allows for cohesive vision and rapid iteration. As development progresses, additional support in areas such as sound design, UI/UX, or asset creation may be considered to enhance specific elements of the game. Collaboration tools like Git for version control, Trello for task management, and Blender for 3D asset creation will be used to support production.

## Tools and Technology
The game uses Unreal Engine 5.5 for development, with supporting tools including Blender for modeling, and Quixel and Megascans for environmental texturing. The project will be managed through GitHub for source control and Trello for milestone tracking. All multiplayer and gameplay logic will be built natively within Unreal’s framework, allowing the project to remain self-contained and scalable.
